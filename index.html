University of Alicante
Welcome

Alicante welcomes the Spain–Japan economic theory community.

The 10th Spain–Japan Meeting on Economic Theory will take place at the University of Alicante on 12–13 November 2026.

The meeting continues the longstanding collaboration between researchers in Spain and Japan working in economic theory, social choice and mechanism design.

Program, participants and social events will be added as they are confirmed. Accommodation and travel information are already available below so participants can begin making arrangements.

Accommodation

Where to stay

Participants can either stay close to the University in San Vicente del Raspeig, within walking distance of the campus, or stay in central Alicante and travel to the University by TRAM. For visitors wishing to enjoy Alicante in the evenings, the city-centre option is particularly convenient.

Travel & local transport

Getting to the University

From Alicante city centre

TRAM · Line L2The simplest option. L2 runs from Luceros through Mercado to Universitat, on the University campus. The University reports a typical frequency of about 15 minutes.
TaxiConvenient for groups or with luggage. Ask for “Universidad de Alicante, campus de San Vicente del Raspeig”.
BusUrban bus line 24 also connects Alicante with the University and San Vicente del Raspeig.

Arriving in Alicante

From Alicante railway stationWalk to Plaza de los Luceros and take TRAM L2 towards Sant Vicent del Raspeig; get off at Universitat.
From Alicante–Elche AirportThe airport is connected to Alicante city by the C6 airport bus and by taxi. From the city centre, continue to the University on TRAM L2.
